# Face Recognition Model Comparison

**Dataset:** JEZT-provided face dataset  
**Evaluation subset:** 240 high-quality images, with up to 30 images per identity  
**Detected faces:** 236  
**Detection failures:** 4  
**Device:** CPU

| Metric | Pretrained pipeline | Fine-tuned pipeline |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy on detected faces | 99.58% | 91.95% |
| Detected faces | 236 | 236 |
| Evaluation images | 240 | 240 |

## Interpretation

The pretrained FaceNet pipeline achieved higher classification accuracy than the partially fine-tuned pipeline on this evaluation sample. Fine-tuning updated the final embedding layers using 160 selected high-quality training faces, while a separate classifier was trained on 240 training embeddings.

The results indicate that this fine-tuning configuration did not improve held-out classification performance. Further investigation is needed to determine whether this is due to limited training data, optimization choices, or generalization limitations.

## Limitations

- The evaluation uses a small, high-quality subset and does not establish performance on the complete dataset.
- Low-quality images require separate evaluation because face detection fails frequently on them.
- Cross-quality duplicate or related-frame leakage has not been completely ruled out.
- Classification accuracy is not the same as verification performance. ROC-AUC, precision-recall, and same-person versus different-person similarity distributions should also be reported.
- Results should be considered preliminary until the split audit and full evaluation are complete.
